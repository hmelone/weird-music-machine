How recommendations work

In short: the machine compares the metadata of records you rate highly with every unheard record, nudged at first by any preferences you saved. Nothing is personalized beyond this browser.

Connections

Shared styles carry 45% of pair similarity, traits 28% and genres 15%. Similar weirdness adds 6%, intensity 4% and accessibility 2%. Within each category, more shared tags and fewer unmatched tags mean a closer connection.

Ratings

A 5-star rating counts twice as much as 4 stars. Three stars are neutral. One- and two-star records push down only candidates whose pair similarity is at least 0.35 and that share musical metadata; one star pushes harder.

Preferences

Before you rate anything 4 or 5 stars, saved preferences rank the picks: records matching your chosen moods and sounds, within your energy range, without the sounds you want less of. After that they only nudge: their weight is 0.3 ÷ (1 + the number of records you have rated), so your ratings take over as they grow.

What is left out

Heard records and records with unresolved scores are never recommended. Your weirdness level, archive filters and any pathway apply to every pick.

Fixed or random

Recommendations are deterministic: the same ratings give the same order, with ties broken by catalog order. With no ratings or preferences, the machine offers a fixed rotation across genres. Surprise Me, and the homepage’s “open door”, pick randomly among unheard records that match your settings.

Weirdness levels

Familiar 1–2, Strange 3, Far Out 4, Completely Unhinged 5. Any opens the whole range, including records whose score is unresolved for Surprise Me.

THE MACHINE / LINER NOTES

THE FAMILIAR
ENDS HERE.

Weird Music Machine began with The Wire’s 100 Records That Set the World on Fire (While No One Was Listening) and its 30 runners-up. The archive now holds 370 releases, with 240 additions from 1969–2026 selected through The Wire, The Quietus and Bandcamp Daily.

Albums, compilations, singles and individual recordings all belong here. Researched genres, styles and traits help you find a way between them.

Your listening log, notes and preferences stay in this browser. Every connection comes from the collection’s existing metadata; the 1–5 musical scores are interpretive guides.
